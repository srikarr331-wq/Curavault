## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Visit **[CuraVault.io](https://app.curavault.io)**
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your details:
   - Email address
   - Full name
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Confirm password
   - Phone number (optional)
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. Check your email for verification link
6. Click the link to verify your email

### Logging In

1. Go to login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. (Optional) Complete 2-factor authentication if enabled

### Resetting Password

1. Click **"Forgot Password"** on login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check email for reset link
4. Click link and enter new password
5. Password is updated immediately

## Patient Guide

### Your Dashboard

The dashboard shows:
- Total number of medical records
- Quick access to recent documents
- Blood group and emergency contacts
- Quick action buttons

### Uploading Medical Records

**To upload a document:**

1. Click **"Upload Report"** button
2. Select report type:
   - Blood Test
   - MRI Scan
   - CT Scan
   - X-Ray
   - Ultrasound
   - ECG
   - Thyroid Report
   - Vaccination Certificate
   - Other
3. Choose file from your computer (PDF, JPG, PNG)
4. Add optional details:
   - Title (required)
   - Description
   - Test date
   - Doctor name
   - Lab name
5. Click **"Upload"**
6. File is encrypted and stored securely

### Managing Your Records

**View all records:**
1. Click **"Medical Records"** in sidebar
2. Browse or search for records
3. Filter by type or date
4. Click record to view details

**Edit record details:**
1. Open the record
2. Click **"Edit"** button
3. Update title, description, doctor/lab names
4. Click **"Save"**

**Delete a record:**
1. Open the record
2. Click **"Delete"** (trash icon)
3. Confirm deletion
4. Record is permanently removed

**Download a record:**
1. Open the record
2. Click **"Download"** button
3. File saved to your computer

### Sharing Records with Doctors

**Generate QR Code for sharing:**

1. Select records you want to share
2. Click **"Generate QR"**
3. Choose access duration:
   - 15 minutes (for in-office visit)
   - 1 hour (for consultation)
   - 24 hours (for home care)
   - 7 days (for ongoing treatment)
4. Click **"Generate"**
5. QR code is displayed and can be printed or shared

**Doctor accesses records:**
1. Doctor scans QR code with phone
2. Your selected records are displayed
3. Doctor can view but not download
4. Access expires after chosen time

**Revoke access:**
1. Go to **"Sharing Requests"**
2. Click **"Revoke"** next to active share
3. Doctor immediately loses access

### Emergency Card

**Set up emergency profile:**

1. Click **"Emergency Card"** in sidebar
2. Fill in critical information:
   - Blood group (required)
   - Allergies
   - Chronic conditions
   - Current medications
   - Emergency contact (name & phone)
3. Click **"Save"**

**Access in emergency:**
- Emergency responders can scan emergency QR code
- Critical information displayed without login
- Information is read-only to emergency personnel

### Health Timeline

Automatically generated timeline showing:
- All medical events chronologically
- Grouping by year and type
- AI-generated summaries

**Features:**
- Filter by year or report type
- Search for specific events
- View trends and patterns
- Export timeline as PDF

### Profile Management

**Edit profile:**

1. Click **"Profile"** → **"Settings"**
2. Update personal information:
   - Full name
   - Phone number
   - Date of birth
   - Gender
   - Blood group
   - Height/Weight
3. Click **"Save"**

**Privacy Settings:**
1. Click **"Privacy"**
2. Control what information is visible to:
   - Doctors you share with
   - Emergency responders
3. Save preferences

### Notifications

**Notification types:**
- Record sharing requests approved
- Access expiration reminders
- New messages from doctors
- Security alerts

**Manage notifications:**
1. Click **"Settings"** → **"Notifications"**
2. Toggle email/in-app notifications
3. Choose notification frequency

### Account Security

**Change password:**
1. Settings → **"Security"**
2. Enter current password
3. Enter new password twice
4. Click **"Update"**

**Enable 2-Factor Authentication:**
1. Settings → **"Security"**
2. Click **"Enable 2FA"**
3. Scan QR code with authenticator app
4. Enter verification code
5. 2FA is now enabled

**View login history:**
1. Settings → **"Security"** → **"Login Activity"**
2. See all login locations and times
3. Click **"Logout All"** to end all sessions

## Doctor Guide

### Accessing Patient Records

**Using QR Code:**
1. Receive QR code from patient
2. Use phone camera or QR app to scan
3. Tap link to access records
4. You are authenticated based on QR code
5. View only shared records

**Searching for patients:**
1. Click **"Find Patient"**
2. Enter patient email or ID
3. (Only if patient has shared with you)
4. View shared records

### Creating Prescriptions

**Add prescription:**
1. Open patient record
2. Click **"New Prescription"**
3. Enter details:
   - Medicine name (required)
   - Dosage (e.g., "500 mg")
   - Frequency (e.g., "Twice daily")
   - Duration (e.g., "7 days")
   - Additional notes
4. Click **"Create"**
5. Prescription is saved to patient's profile

### Consultation Notes

**Add notes:**
1. Open patient record
2. Click **"Add Consultation Note"**
3. Enter consultation details
4. Attach related reports if needed
5. Click **"Save"**

### Patient Communication

**Send message to patient:**
1. Open patient record
2. Click **"Messages"**
3. Type message
4. Click **"Send"**
5. Patient receives email notification

## Hospital Admin Guide

### Managing Doctors

**Add doctor:**
1. Click **"Doctors"** in admin panel
2. Click **"Add Doctor"**
3. Enter details:
   - Full name
   - Email
   - License number
   - Specialization
   - Department
4. Click **"Create"**
5. Doctor receives invite email

**Verify doctors:**
1. Doctors list shows pending verification
2. Review license number
3. Click **"Approve"** or **"Reject"**

### Organization Settings

**Update hospital info:**
1. Click **"Settings"** → **"Organization"**
2. Update:
   - Hospital name
   - Address
   - Phone
   - Email
   - Logo (optional)
3. Click **"Save"**

### Analytics & Reports

**View usage analytics:**
1. Click **"Analytics"**
2. See metrics:
   - Total doctors
   - Total patient records uploaded
   - Records shared this month
   - Most used report types
3. Export as PDF or CSV

### Audit Logs

**View activity logs:**
1. Click **"Audit Logs"**
2. See all activities:
   - Login events
   - Record uploads
   - Sharing activities
3. Filter by date, user, or action type
4. Export logs for compliance

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes! All data is encrypted with AES-256 encryption. We comply with HIPAA and international data protection standards.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes. Go to Settings → Account and click "Delete Account". All your data will be permanently removed.

**Q: How long are backups kept?**
A: We keep daily backups for 90 days for disaster recovery purposes.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What file formats are supported?**
A: PDF, JPEG, PNG. Maximum file size is 50 MB.

**Q: Can I export all my records?**
A: Yes, click Settings → Export Data to download all records as a ZIP file.

**Q: Is there an offline mode?**
A: Currently no, but this is planned for v2.0.

### Privacy Questions

**Q: Can doctors download my records?**
A: Only if you explicitly share them. They cannot download; they can only view shared records.

**Q: Who can see my emergency card?**
A: Only emergency responders with the emergency QR code can access it.

**Q: Can I see who accessed my records?**
A: Yes, go to Settings → Access Logs to see all access history.
